Privacy and data processing

Updated October 23, 2026. Website operator: Autónoma Dev project operator, Argentina. Contact for privacy requests: the site's inquiry form, starting the message with “Privacy”.

What we collect

Contact name, email, optional company name, country, preferred quote currency, requested service, approximate budget, project description, consent timestamp and basic technical information to reduce abusive traffic.

Why we collect it

We use the information to evaluate and respond to your inquiry, prepare potential proposals and protect the inquiry form. We do not sell personal data or automatically subscribe you to marketing. Submitting the form does not create a contract or trigger a charge.

Where we store it

Inquiries are stored using Neon PostgreSQL infrastructure. The public website is hosted on Render. These service providers may process data outside your country, including outside Argentina or the European Economic Area. Do not submit passwords, identity documents or sensitive information in the project description.

Retention and your choices

We retain inquiries only as long as needed for handling requests and applicable legal requirements. Precise deletion periods are still being formalized. You may request access, correction or deletion when applicable by sending a “Privacy” inquiry. We may ask you to verify identity before sharing data. This page is an initial notice, not a finalized compliance assessment for every jurisdiction.

International privacy

Cross-border transfers and privacy obligations vary by location. Additional privacy disclosures and processing agreements may be required before larger or sensitive projects can be accepted.
